Midnight Escape

Welcome to Midnight Escape

You wake up in a mysterious town at midnight. The streets are empty, and something is watching you. Can you escape before it's too late?

How to Play

Read the story and choose what to do by clicking the buttons. Your choices determine your fate. Atmospheric music starts after your first choice. You can turn sound off at any time.
