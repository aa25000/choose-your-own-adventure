The town is waiting.

You wake up in a deserted town at midnight. Its streets are empty. Something is watching you. Can you escape?

How to play

Read each scene and pick one of two choices. Explore different routes and try again to find new ways out. Click the story to reveal it instantly.

Reach an ending before time runs out. Medium is recommended for a first playthrough. Pause any time with the Pause button or the P key.

Music begins after you interact with the game. Sound can be muted anytime.
